# Percolation and density-floor descriptors report

`percolation_descriptors.md` is the report on `rho_perc_a/b/c`, `perc_anisotropy` and
`rho_min_ratio`, with the `paw` extension's `rho_min_int_ratio`. It covers their
definitions, formulas, implementation, physical meaning, analytic behaviour, where the
bottlenecks and minima lie, numerical robustness, the dependence on the choice of cell, ML
predictability and a survey over the 6,059-structure VASP dataset.

- `figures/`: 9 figures, PNG (200 dpi) and PDF (vector).
- `data/`: every table behind the figures and numbers.
- `scripts/`: the scripts that regenerate them. The order and inputs are in section 12 of
  the report.
